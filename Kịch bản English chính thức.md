# PRESENTATION SCRIPT: THE DANGERS OF READING BOOKS

* **Duration:** 30 minutes

* **Tone & Style:** Dramatic, exaggerated, reverse sarcasm (talking like a serious emergency news reporter or a doctor warning about a dangerous disease, while actually praising books).

* **English Level:** A1–A2 (Simple words, short sentences, easy to speak and understand).

* **Stage Notes:** `[...]` shows body language, voice tone, and slide changes.

## INTRODUCTION: EMERGENCY WARNING (3 minutes)

**\[Slide 1: The Dangers of Reading Books\]**
*(Walk onto the stage with a very serious face. Take a deep breath. Look at the audience like you are about to share a shocking secret).*

"Hello everyone. Today, I am not here to inspire you. I am here to give you an emergency warning about a very dangerous thing!

It is legal. You can buy it anywhere. You do not eat it, and you do not drink it. It enters your body through your eyes. Then, it attacks your brain! It changes who you are. Once you start using it, your life will never be the same again.

What is this terrible danger? It is... **READING BOOKS!**"

**\[Slide 2: 30-Minute Warning Plan\]**
*(Change to a strong, serious news-reporter voice).*

"In the next 30 minutes, I will show you the dark truth about reading books in 5 parts:

1. Crazy and scary numbers about book lovers.

2. Three dangerous tools: Paper books, E-books, and Audiobooks.

3. Seven terrible 'dangers' that change your brain forever.

4. Why 90% of people are lucky that they quit reading early.

5. And finally, for brave people who still want to take the risk: How to get 'infected' by books in the best way!"

## PART 1: CRAZY NUMBERS & STRANGE FACTS (5 minutes)

**\[Slide 3: Part 01 - Crazy Numbers\]**
"Let's start with science. Do not think reading is safe. Scientists around the world were shocked when they found these numbers."

**\[Slide 4: Numbers That Speak\]**
*(Point at the number 68%).*

"First: **68%**! A study shows that reading for just 6 minutes reduces stress by 68%. That is faster than listening to music, drinking tea, or walking! Do you see the problem here? It makes your brain too calm! Your boss yells at you, and you just smile. Your boyfriend or girlfriend gets angry, and you stay relaxed. Traffic is terrible, and you feel peaceful. Reading steals your right to get mad like a normal person!

Second, doctors have a name for a strange sickness from Japan: **Tsundoku**. This means buying mountains of books, and never reading them! Sick people see a book sale, and their hands automatically click 'Buy'. They put the books on a shelf just to look smart. This proves one big truth: *Buying books and reading books are two completely different hobbies!*

And the third number is truly scary: **+2 Years**! Scientists found that people who read 30 minutes a day live 2 years longer than non-readers. Oh no! Think about the money! Living 2 more years means paying for 2 more years of food, 2 more years of electricity, 2 more years of taxes, and 2 more years of work!"

## PART 2: THREE DANGEROUS TOOLS (5 minutes)

**\[Slide 5: Part 02 - 3 Popular Tools\]**
"To attack us everywhere, books now come in 3 different forms."

**\[Slide 6: Comparing 3 Formats\]**
*(Speak like a military expert).*

"First is the **Paper Book** — a classic weapon. It smells amazing, and the sound of turning pages makes people addicted. But what is the side effect? Put two thick books in your backpack, and you are carrying free gym weights all day. Goodbye, straight back!

Second is the **E-book Reader** — a quiet, light killer. It weighs only 200 grams, but it hides a whole library in your pocket. With a screen light, it can attack you even in the dark! However, it has one big weakness: Imagine reading a mystery story. You are on the final page. The killer is about to show his face... and the screen says: *'Low Battery!'* Pure pain!

Third is the **Audiobook** — an attack through your ears. It catches you while you wash dishes, drive a car, or run. The biggest danger? Nice, warm voices become strong sleeping pills. Many victims turn on an audiobook at 10 PM. At 6 AM, they wake up. The book is finished, and they remember zero words!"

## PART 3: 7 TERRIBLE "DANGERS" OF READING (10 minutes - Main Part)

**\[Slide 7: Part 03 - 7 Most Dangerous Effects\]**
*(Lower your voice. Sound mysterious and dramatic).*

"Now, take a deep breath. I will tell you the 7 worst things that happen to your life when you read books!"

**\[Slide 8: Mind & Sleep Problems (1–4)\]**
"Danger number 1: **Less stress and better sleep — so you sleep forever!**
Books relax your mind so well that after a few pages, your eyes close and you sleep deeply. Think about it! If you sleep early and sleep well every night, who will stay up all night to watch Korean dramas? Who will scroll social media until 3 AM to read celebrity gossip? What a waste of youth!

Danger number 2: **Knowing too much!**
Reading puts too much information into your head. You lose your cute, innocent mind. When everyone on the internet panics about fake news, you sit quietly and see the logical mistakes — just because you read about it before. Suddenly, you are the strange one in the group!

Danger number 3: **A dangerously big vocabulary!**
Normal people see a pretty view and say: *'Wow, so cool!'* Heavy readers? They use big, poetic words, and their friends think they came from the 19th century! Worse, their sharp words make them a nightmare in arguments. Fighting with a book reader is like throwing an egg at a rock!

Danger number 4: **A wild, uncontrollable imagination!**
From black words on white paper, a reader's brain builds a 4K movie with amazing special effects for free! Why is this sad? Because when Hollywood makes a real movie from that book, the reader walks out of the cinema very disappointed: *'The movie was boring. It looked nothing like the movie in my head!'*"

**\[Slide 9: Life & Personality Problems (5–7)\]**
*(Clap your hands loudly to move to the next 3 dangers).*

"It does not stop at your brain! These last 3 dangers will destroy your wallet, your criminal career, and your daily habits!

Danger number 5: **A domino disaster for your money and free time!** Look at this sad chain: You read books -> You get addicted to buying books -> You waste a lot of money to buy books - Books cover your floor, so you pay big money for a bookshelf -> Now you have a bookshelf, you have to clean dust off every shelf! Reading is the fastest way to waste your money and give yourself extra housework!

Danger number 6: **You can never become a professional thief!**
There is a famous story about a bookstore that stayed open all night. There were no workers, and the security cameras were off. The next morning, zero books were stolen! People made a famous rule from this: ***'Readers never steal, and thieves never read.'*** Do you see? Reading makes you too kind and too honest. You are banned forever from joining the 'thief club'!

And danger number 7 — the most embarrassing one: **Hiding in the... bathroom all day!**
Scientists discovered a 'Mini Library called... the Toilet'. A survey showed that **46% of men** and **26% of women** often take books, newspapers, or comics into the bathroom! The story gets so good that they forget the time. They turn the toilet into a VIP reading room, while the whole family stands outside knocking on the door for help!"

## PART 4: WHY 90% OF PEOPLE QUIT READING (4 minutes)

**\[Slide 10: Why Do 90% of Readers Quit Halfway?\]**
*(Change your voice to sound friendly, understanding, and funny).*

"After hearing these 7 terrible dangers, many of you are feeling happy right now: *'Thank goodness! I always quit after 3 pages!'* Congratulations! You are part of the 90% of humans who escaped safely, thanks to 3 reasons:

First: **The 'Fake Smart Person' disease!** You see people take cool photos at a coffee shop with books. You want to look deep and smart too, so you buy one. You read 3 pages, your brain shuts down from confusion, and that expensive book becomes... a stand for your computer monitor!

Second: **Losing the war against the '15-Second Empire'!**
Smartphones and 15-second short videos give your brain fast excitement. Every swipe brings music, dancing, and laughs. Next to that fast machine, quiet black-and-white book pages look as slow as an old turtle!

Third: **Becoming a 'slave' to the book!**
Many people think: *'I paid 150,000 dong for this book, so I must read every single word!'* Even when the book is super boring, you force yourself to keep going. You turn a fun hobby into... mental torture!"

## PART 5: 4 STEPS TO GET "INFECTED" THE RIGHT WAY (3 minutes)

**\[Slide 11: 4 Steps to Get "Infected" Properly\]**
*(Speak with an encouraging, playful, inviting tone).*

"However, after all my scary warnings today, maybe some brave warriors in this room still want to take the risk and start reading. If that is you, here are 4 easy tricks to get 'infected' happily:

**Step 1: Play the 'Trick of the Century' on your brain (The 5-Page Rule).**
You must fool your own brain! Never shout: *'Today I will read 50 pages!'* Your brain's alarm system will ring loudly and say NO immediately. Instead, act innocent and whisper a sweet trap to yourself: *'Today I will read only 5 pages. Then I will stop!'* Five pages is so small that your brain relaxes and falls into the trap. Once you pass page 5, the story pulls you in, and you keep turning pages without even noticing!

**Step 2: Eat dessert first, eat vegetables later.**
Do not start with heavy business or philosophy books. Start with exciting detective stories, funny novels, or comics — anything you truly enjoy! Get addicted to turning pages first.

**Step 3: Give yourself the right to 'break up with no reason' (The 50-Page Rule).**
Reading is not a university exam! If you read 50 pages and you do not like the book, close it, say goodbye without feeling guilty, and pick a new one. Life is too short for boring books!

**Step 4: Collect 'secret weapons' for fun parties and late-night *deeptalks*.**
Do not read with stress, and do not try to memorize everything right away. Just let the words sink in slowly, like a quiet addiction. Maybe not today, maybe not tomorrow, but one day, you will suddenly surprise yourself! Smart ideas and great words from your books will naturally pop out of your mouth. They will become the secret 'spice' that makes every party more fun and makes your late-night *deeptalks* with friends unforgettable. And in that magical moment — congratulations, you can never quit reading again!"

## CONCLUSION & Q&A: AN EVIL BLESSING (2 minutes)

**\[Slide 12: Final Advice\]**
*(Step forward on the stage. Give a big, warm, genuine smile).*

"Ladies and gentlemen,
Reading books is truly dangerous. Because it will kill the old, small version of you from yesterday, and build a new version of you — deeper, funnier, kinder, and stronger.

Thank you for bravely sitting through this 30-minute warning. Today, I want to give you all a very 'evil' wish: **I hope you catch the reading 'disease' soon, and never find a cure!**

Thank you, and now I am ready for your questions!"
